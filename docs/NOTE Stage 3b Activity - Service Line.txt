The final stage routes activity costs to Service Lines.

Note: Drivers were already loaded in Stage 1a. 
Stage 3 uses the settled activity costs from Stage 2c output.

How it works:

1. Each activity's costs are apportioned to the Service 
   Lines it supports, based on driver percentages.

2. Service Lines represent the final cost objects — 
   typically products, services, or cost centres.

3. The apportionment formula:
   
   Apportioned Amount = Activity Amount x Driver %
   
   Example:
     HR Activity: $150,000 total costs
     Service Line: Employee Benefits driver %
     Product A: 40%
     Product B: 35%
     Product C: 25%
     
     Product A receives: $150,000 x 40% = $60,000
     Product B receives: $150,000 x 35% = $52,500
     Product C receives: $150,000 x 25% = $37,500
